How to Play

Goal: each round has a score goal. Build domino chains until your round score breaches it. Fail with no plays left and the run is over.

Chains: tap dominoes in your hand to lay them left-to-right (up to 4 slots to start). Tap a placed domino to flip it. You need at least 2 dominoes to play a chain.

Scoring (BIDMAS): the two exposed outer ends are added; every pair of touching ends is multiplied. So (1|2)(3|4)(5|6)(6|1) scores 1 + 2×3 + 4×5 + 6×6 + 1 = 64.

Power ends (LEGENDARY): a legendary upgrade turns an end's operator from × into ^. A joint touching a power end scores aᵇ instead of a×b — powers bind before multiplication, exactly as BIDMAS demands. Powered dominoes are exclusive: they can't be duplicated, the powered end's pips are locked forever, they can't be sealed, and each domino can hold only one power end.

Seals: a Ruby Seal makes every joint a domino touches retrigger — its base value scores again.

Plays & discards: 3 plays per round; scoring a chain consumes its dominoes for the round and refills your hand. Discards let you swap up to 5 hand dominoes, 3 times per round.

Money: clear a round to earn $4, plus $1 per unused play, plus interest ($1 per $5 held). Spend it in the Bone Bazaar on charms, new dominoes, pip upgrades, power ends and vouchers.

Materials: Gold dominoes pay $1 whenever they score. Crystal dominoes double every joint they touch — but may shatter after scoring.

Bosses: every 3rd round a boss twists the rules. Read the banner.
